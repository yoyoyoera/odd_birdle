Delete my Odd Birdle data

This page erases the daily-challenge record, nickname and account that the Odd Birdle app keeps for you on our server. You do not need the app, and you do not need to sign in: you only need your delete code.

Find it in the app under DAILY → your name → SHOW DELETE CODE (twelve letters and numbers, like K7M2-QX9D-4HTB).

What is erased

Your daily clears and their places, your nickname, your sign-in link (Apple or Google) and the records of the devices you used. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to end the link. It cannot be undone. Your high-score places on past days disappear from the daily boards.

Lost your code?

Write to verejohn21@gmail.com from the account you signed in with and say which sign-in (Apple or Google) you used. We will confirm it is you and erase the data.

삭제 안내 (한국어)

앱에서 DAILY → 내 이름 → SHOW DELETE CODE로 보이는 삭제 코드(영문·숫자 12자)를 위 칸에 입력하면, 서버에 있는 데일리 기록, 닉네임, 계정 연결이 모두 지워지고 되돌릴 수 없습니다. 앱을 지운 뒤에도 같은 방법으로 지울 수 있습니다. 코드를 잃어버렸다면 verejohn21@gmail.com로 로그인한 방식(Apple/Google)을 적어 요청해 주세요.

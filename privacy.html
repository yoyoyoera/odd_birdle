Odd Birdle app — Privacy policy

Applies to the Odd Birdle app for Android and iPhone. Last updated: November 25, 2026.

In short

The app does not collect, send or share any personal information. It has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it works without an internet connection.

What stays on your device

The app keeps a few settings on your device only (for example, sound on or off and touch speed). They are never sent anywhere. Removing the app removes them.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.
  • It contains no advertising and no third-party tracking.
  • It does not connect to any server in this version.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a later version adds online features (such as rankings or online matches), this page will be updated before that version is released, and will say what is stored and how to delete it.

Contact

Odd Birdle is made by studiyo. Questions about this policy: studiyo.pages.dev.

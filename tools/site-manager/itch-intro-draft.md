# Odd Birdle: a strange little badminton game

Odd Birdle is now playable on itch.io! It's a quick 1v1 badminton game where the shuttle doesn't always look—or behave—the way you expect.

Play against five CPU difficulty levels, find an opponent with Quick Match, or invite a friend using a room code. Strange shuttle shapes and random effects can turn a familiar rally into a very different challenge.

Move your racket with your mouse or touch. A faster swing hits harder. Keep the shuttle in play, watch where it lands, and adapt when things get weird.

Try it here: https://studiyo21.itch.io/odd-birdle

I'd love to hear which shuttle shape or random effect surprised you most. Thanks for playing!
